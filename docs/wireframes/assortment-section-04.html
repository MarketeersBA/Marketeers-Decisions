2

WD push criteria Rule proposal

Arrows appear when a SKU earns shelf space (positive incremental share) but is under-distributed relative to its sales efficiency.

① Greedy introduction order
At step k, from remaining brand SKUs, add the one with highest Δ sharecumulative when introduced on top of SKUs already selected in steps 1…k−1.
② Product Sales Rate (PSR)
PSR = Value Sales ÷ WD — reflects how much value sales increase per 1 WD point. Requires WD > 0 in the data feed.
③ Push WD arrow (proposed)
Show green ↑ when all of:
  • SKU is in the introduction sequence (not delisted / excluded)
  • PSR ≥ median(PSR) across brand SKUs in current filter
  • WD < WDref − 10 pp where WDref = highest WD among SKUs already introduced or the brand anchor SKU (step 1)
Label: “Push WD to match [anchor SKU]” when gap is vs anchor; otherwise “Push — high PSR, low WD”.
Example in chart above
43" has the largest share jump (+41 pp) but WD trails 32" HD → push to match anchor. 50" has PSR 2.1 (above median) with WD below reference → push despite small final share increment. 49" has low PSR (0.6) → no arrow even though it is in the lineup.

Discussion points for client

  1. ① Cumulative introduction from empty lineup Confirm greedy max-jump ordering is the right default. Should users be able to override the sequence, or lock certain SKUs as mandatory first picks?
  2. ② WD + Product Sales Rate overlay Do we have reliable WD and value sales in the study feed for all clients? If WD is missing, hide PSR line and push arrows.
  3. ③ Push WD criteria Proposed rule: PSR ≥ median AND WD at least 10 pp below anchor (step-1 SKU or highest-WD intro). Is 10 pp the right gap? Should anchor always be step 1 or highest WD in category?
  4. ④ Recommended assortment table Map intro step + push flags to Keep / Watch / Push WD. Delist is out of scope for introduction-only flow.
  5. Scope for next sprint Build ① greedy intro chart with WD + PSR overlays; derive ③ table and headline from same run.
